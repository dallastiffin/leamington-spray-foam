What Helps Us Quote

The space and roughly how big it is. An attic, a crawl space, a basement, a garage, a packhouse.

What it does wrong, and when. A room that is cold in February is a different problem to a smell that shows up in July, and knowing which one you have changes the answer.

Whether water has ever come into the space, and when it last happened.

For a crawl space, the headroom and the size of the hatch. That drives the price more than the area does.

Photos. Half the time a photo tells us more than the phone call, especially of an attic hatch, a rim joist or insulation hanging out of joists.
